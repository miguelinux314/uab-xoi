\section*{AI Usage}

\subsection*{Recommended AI usage}

\noindent AI is an exciting tool but, as of today (2026-27 academic year), it struggles to answer correctly many of the important questions in this course.
We encourage a responsible use of AI to organize and work with the materials, but we \textbf{completely advise against using AI to solve exercises, missions and labs}.

\noindent AI may be used to:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Understand:} the requirements and instructions.
\item \textbf{Explore:} ask questions about content, concepts and ideas.
\item \textbf{Summarise:} paraphrase or break down complex ideas.
\item \textbf{Source:} find references to support your work.
\item \textbf{Code:} speed up curiosity and testing, not solve labs or missions.
\item \textbf{Spelling and grammar:} check and correct text.
\item \textbf{Debug:} get feedback on code written by a human or AI.
\end{itemize}
